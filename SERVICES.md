# Pullupsell Partners — Service Offerings & Niche Customization

**Document Purpose:** Define your three core products and how to adapt messaging for different niches (HVAC, roofing, real estate, etc.)

---

## 📋 Overview

Pullupsell Partners sells **three AI-powered solutions** that solve different problems for local service businesses.

**Key Principle:** The **problem** changes by niche, but the **solution** structure stays the same.

Example adaptations:
- HVAC company → "More recall customers in winter"
- Roofer → "More qualified leads during storm season"
- Real estate → "More referrals from past clients"

---

## 🎯 Product 1: Photo Quote Engine

### Core Idea
Prospects upload photos → System creates structured estimate request → Sales team gets qualified leads.

### Standard Positioning

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Outcome** | More qualified quote requests from visitors who aren't ready to call |
| **Buyer** | Contractors, roofers, paving, signage, fencing, painting |
| **Promise** | "Visitors upload photos, the app creates an estimate request, and your team gets structured leads instead of vague emails" |
| **Problem Solved** | Unqualified phone calls, lost leads from hesitant customers |
| **Setup Cost** | $750–$2,000 |
| **Monthly** | $150–$500 (support/reporting) |

### Niche Customization Template

| Niche | Pain Point | Customized Promise | Buyer Type |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Roofing** | Missing urgent leads during storm season | "Homeowners upload damage photos → You get verified roof jobs ready to quote" | Roofers, contractors |
| **HVAC** | AC repairs from emergency calls at night | "Customers describe AC issues via photo → You filter urgent jobs before bed" | HVAC companies |
| **Painting** | Can't estimate paint jobs over email | "Clients send room photos → Precise quotes auto-generated → Zero back-and-forth" | Painting contractors |
| **Real Estate** | Agents losing leads to hesitation | "Sellers send property photos → Instant valuation estimate → Lead captured" | Real estate agents |
| **Plumbing** | Emergency calls with unclear scope | "Customers photo their leak → You dispatch right tech with right parts" | Plumbing companies |
| **Landscaping** | Losing yard projects to competitors | "Homeowners photo yard → Instant design mockup → 3x more bookings" | Landscapers |

### How to Customize for Your Niche

**Step 1: Identify the pain**
- What's the top reason leads DON'T convert now?
- (Hesitation, unclear scope, timing, price shock?)

**Step 2: Rewrite the promise**
- **Generic:** "Get structured leads"
- **HVAC-specific:** "Capture emergency HVAC jobs before they go to competitors"
- **Roofing-specific:** "Turn storm damage photos into verified roof jobs"

**Step 3: Show ROI**
- **Generic:** "More leads"
- **HVAC-specific:** "3x more recall customers in peak season"
- **Real estate-specific:** "Reduce lead response time from 24hrs to 2 mins"

## 🎯 Product 2: Repeat Booking Engine

### Core Idea
Past customers get flagged when due for service → Sales team gets ready-to-send reminders → More repeat business.

### Standard Positioning

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Outcome** | More repeat customers without staff manually chasing them |
| **Buyer** | HVAC, dental, pest control, grooming, med spas, maintenance |
| **Promise** | "Past customers get flagged when they're due, and your team gets ready-to-send reminders" |
| **Problem Solved** | Forgotten customers, lost recurring revenue, manual follow-up overhead |
| **Setup Cost** | $500–$1,500 |
| **Monthly** | $100–$300 (maintenance) |

### Niche Customization Template

| Niche | Service Cycle | Customized Promise | Buyer Type |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **HVAC** | Spring AC tune-up, fall heater check | "Customers due for AC tune-up? System reminds them. Your team sends one reminder. 60% book again." | HVAC companies |
| **Dental** | 6-month cleanings | "Your dental patients get due-date reminders → 40% more recall appointments" | Dental offices |
| **Pest Control** | Quarterly treatments | "Past customers auto-flagged quarterly → 3x more annual contracts renewed" | Pest control companies |
| **Med Spa** | Monthly facials, quarterly injectables | "Customers notified when due for next treatment → 50% higher repeat rate" | Medical spas |
| **Grooming** | 6–8 week haircut cycles | "Dog owners reminded when Fido is due for grooming → More regular appointments" | Grooming salons |
| **Maintenance** | Seasonal inspections | "Building owners reminded of required maintenance → Predictable recurring revenue" | Maintenance companies |

### How to Customize for Your Niche

**Step 1: Identify the cycle**
- How often should customers return? (Monthly? Quarterly? Seasonally?)
- What's your current repeat rate? (20%? 50%? 80%?)

**Step 2: Rewrite the promise**
- **Generic:** "More repeat customers"
- **HVAC-specific:** "Your HVAC customers book spring AC tune-ups + fall heater checks automatically"
- **Dental-specific:** "6-month cleanings scheduled automatically → Zero missed recall patients"

**Step 3: Show ROI**
- **Generic:** "No manual follow-up"
- **HVAC-specific:** "Recover $XX in lost seasonal maintenance revenue"
- **Med spa-specific:** "Increase client lifetime value by $XXX"

## 🎯 Product 3: Local Prospect Hunter

### Core Idea
Pick a service area + customer type → AI finds likely buyers + drafts outreach → Sales team approves + sends.

### Standard Positioning

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Outcome** | Fresh list of scored local prospects with custom outreach angles |
| **Buyer** | B2B local service providers |
| **Promise** | "Pick a service area and customer type. The app finds likely buyers and drafts outreach your team can approve" |
| **Problem Solved** | Outdated leads, generic outreach, low response rates |
| **Setup Cost** | $1,000–$3,000 |
| **Monthly** | $300–$1,000 (ongoing prospecting) |

### Niche Customization Template

| Niche | Buyer Type | Customized Promise | Outreach Angle |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Commercial HVAC** | Office buildings, warehouses | "Find commercial buildings in [ZIP] needing HVAC service contracts" | Energy savings, compliance, downtime risk |
| **B2B Roofing** | Retail chains, warehouses, apartment complexes | "Find commercial properties with aging roofs in [ZIP]" | Liability, insurance, weather damage |
| **Commercial Plumbing** | Restaurants, laundries, factories | "Find businesses in [ZIP] with aging plumbing infrastructure" | Emergency prevention, code violations |
| **Janitorial** | Office buildings, retail spaces, schools | "Find facilities in [ZIP] using outdated cleaning services" | Health codes, staff retention, reputation |
| **Pest Control (B2B)** | Restaurants, warehouses, retail | "Find food businesses in [ZIP] needing pest management contracts" | Health inspection risk, customer complaints |
| **Commercial Electrical** | Warehouses, factories, offices | "Find businesses in [ZIP] with aging electrical systems" | Code compliance, fire risk, downtime prevention |

### How to Customize for Your Niche

**Step 1: Define your ideal target**
- What size company? (1–10 employees? 50–500 people?)
- What industry? (Restaurants? Tech offices? Warehouses?)
- What problem do they have? (Aging systems? Compliance? Cost?)

**Step 2: Rewrite the promise**
- **Generic:** "Find local prospects"
- **Commercial HVAC:** "AI finds office buildings + warehouses in your ZIP with aging HVAC systems + drafts cold emails"
- **Janitorial:** "Find 50 facilities in [ZIP] that need better cleaning service + we draft the outreach"

**Step 3: Show ROI**
- **Generic:** "Fresh leads"
- **Commercial HVAC:** "Save 40 hours/month on prospecting. Close 2-3 additional contracts → $50K extra revenue"
- **Janitorial:** "Turn $500/month prospecting cost into $5K/month in new contracts"
